# PaperMax privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

PaperMax is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows PC. There is no PaperMax server, account or analytics: nothing you do in PaperMax is sent to the makers of PaperMax.

## Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive (to send books to a Kindle running KOReader), PaperMax asks Google for the `drive.file` permission only. With it, PaperMax can see and change only the files it created itself, kept in one folder named "PaperMax for KOReader". It cannot read, list or change any other file in your Drive.

What PaperMax keeps in that folder: the books you choose to send (EPUB files), a list of those books, and one small file per Kindle with its reading progress, finished books and highlights.

How the data is used: only to move your books to your Kindle and your progress and highlights back into PaperMax on your PC. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where the permission is kept: Google's permission token is stored encrypted on your PC (protected by Windows), and a copy is written to the Kindle you link so it can sync on its own. It never leaves your devices except to talk to Google.

How to stop: press "disconnect" on PaperMax's KOREADER screen, or remove PaperMax at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. The files in the "PaperMax for KOReader" folder stay in your Drive until you delete them.

PaperMax's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Everything else

Your library (articles, highlights, tags, settings) is stored on your PC. Sign-ins you make inside PaperMax for websites are stored encrypted on your PC and are used only to read those websites for you.
