# PaperMax

PaperMax is a read-it-later app for Windows. It keeps the articles you save, finds new writing from the websites you follow, and makes a magazine of what you have not read yet.

It can send articles and magazine issues to a Kindle running KOReader through your own Google Drive, and bring your reading progress and highlights back. For that, PaperMax asks Google for access to **only the files it creates itself** (the `drive.file` permission), in one folder named "PaperMax for KOReader". It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
